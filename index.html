    What gets removed, and what stays

    Removed

    • Location: GPS position and altitude, city, country
    • Device: camera / phone make, model, serial and lens numbers, maker notes
    • Identity: artist, owner, author, last-modified-by, company, manager, comment authors
    • Timestamps: taken, edited, printed, total editing time
    • History: software, edit steps, revision numbers, templates
    • Hidden content: embedded thumbnails, data after the image, AI prompts

    Always kept

    • Your content: pixels and document text are never re-encoded
    • Orientation: so photos don't turn sideways
    • Colour profile: so colours look the same
    • AI disclosure: C2PA content credentials and the IPTC "AI-generated" label. MetaScrub protects people, not the fact that an image was made by AI.
